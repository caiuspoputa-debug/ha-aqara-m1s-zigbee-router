Aqara M1S Zigbee Coordinator + Router v0.30.0 validation

Base: v0.3.0, derived from the established v0.21.13 Router integration.

Validated package behavior:
- The internal domain remains aqara_m1s_zigbee_router for in-place upgrades.
- Coordinator/Router behavior is selected from the runtime role, never from an IP address.
- Router hubs retain Rejoin, ring RGB and illuminance entities.
- Coordinator hubs expose Coordinator ON/OFF and suppress Router Rejoin, RGB and lux UART traffic.
- The last confirmed role is persisted for safe Home Assistant startup while a hub is offline.
- Sound deletion accepts any WAV below /data/musics; upload remains restricted to /data/musics/music-ch.
- Sound deletion still requires explicit confirmation and creates a mandatory hub-side backup first.
- The v0.21.15 20 ms physical-button polling migration is restored.
- The redundant Refresh Sound List button entity is not created and stale registry entries are removed.
- No sound is selected by default; explicit confirmation is required.
- A hub-side archive is mandatory before selected sounds are deleted.

Hardware evidence on Aqara M1S 192.168.0.107:
- Exact JN5189 flash and 47,416-byte readback passed.
- Spinel 4.3 and unmodified zigbee-on-host handshake passed.
- Zigbee network formation on channel 20 and MAC transmission passed.
- Coordinator ON/OFF and persistence across a controlled hub reboot passed.
- Telnet, HomeKit, audio/MQTT, Wi-Fi recovery and button services remained active.
