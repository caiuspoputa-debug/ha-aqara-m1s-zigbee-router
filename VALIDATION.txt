WAV catalog and deletion guarantees:
- /data/musics/music-us and every nested WAV are excluded from list_sounds().
- Hidden music-us sounds therefore do not become Home Assistant sound buttons,
  do not appear in the multi-delete selector and stale buttons are pruned after
  a successful catalog refresh or integration reload.
- Python path validation rejects deletion and managed upload paths below
  /data/musics/music-us before any command is sent to the hub.
- The on-hub deletion command independently rejects data/musics/music-us/*.
- Other confirmed WAV selections are deleted directly. No tar command and no
  /data/m1s_sound_backups archive is created.
- The complete manifest is preflighted before deletion and the reported deleted
  count must equal the confirmed selection.

WAV upload storage guarantees:
- Every WAV/ZIP batch is validated in full before its first file is written.
- Existing destination sizes are subtracted when replacement growth is
  calculated.
- The projected result must retain at least 8 MiB free on /data; otherwise the
  complete batch is rejected and no upload starts.
- The direct upload_sound service uses the same preflight and reserve.
