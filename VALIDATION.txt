Audio behavior added in 0.37.0:
- Individual and group entities report BUFFERING during initial prefill and
  source recovery instead of reporting false active playback.
- Individual playout rebases expose count, lag, last TCP drain duration and a
  likely cause: TCP timeout, slow TCP drain or Home Assistant scheduler/await.
- Group media requests use latest-request-wins generations before and after
  asynchronous media-source and title resolution.
- Group Stop and hard reset release all individual players suspended by the
  group, unless a newer group request already superseded that shutdown.
- A group receiver is rebuilt independently after three consecutive stale ALSA
  samples at five-second intervals. One sample never triggers a rebuild.
- Restored playback prefers the complete selected cohort for at most 30 seconds,
  then starts the available hubs and lets missing hubs use late join.
- Manual group Play uses a one-second initial cohort grace window.
- Adaptive synchronization, per-hub resampling and periodic automatic receiver
  resync remain disabled. One shared PCM history and one common playout clock
  remain authoritative.

Coordinator unload safety added in 0.37.1:
- Integration unload, reload and update close only local Coordinator sockets.
- The persistent Coordinator TCP/UART relay on port 1886 is never stopped.
- Temporary Router sideband UART tunnels retain their existing cleanup.

Availability regression coverage retained from 0.36.9:
- Hub Connectivity is online when either MQTT IO or Telnet independently proves
  the physical hub reachable, and offline only when both paths are down.
- The sensor exposes connection_source without coupling general availability to
  optional MQTT-only diagnostics.
- Retained state cannot override retained offline availability during reconnect.
- Hub Connectivity and dependent CoordinatorEntity controls transition together.

WAV upload and playback guarantees retained:
- Configure accepts exactly one WAV per operation; ZIP is not accepted.
- Capacity preflight runs before progress and preserves at least 8 MiB on /data.
- The dedicated transfer client prevents normal polling from holding the upload.
- WAV validation requires PCM mono, 32000 Hz, signed 32-bit little-endian and a
  maximum file size of 20 MiB.
- Transfer success requires matching remote size and MD5.
- Stored WAV priority still suspends media, plays the WAV, then restores the
  remembered individual or group playback.
- MQTT transports only receiver prepare/stop commands, never radio PCM.

No JN5189 firmware write or flash is performed by this integration package.
