- All 43 isolated regression tests pass.

WAV upload guarantees:
- Configure accepts exactly one WAV file per operation. ZIP archives are not
  offered or accepted.
- The file is validated as uncompressed PCM, mono, 32000 Hz, 32-bit
  little-endian and is limited to 20 MiB.
- A short single-file capacity command accounts for an existing destination
  as a replacement and keeps at least 8 MiB free on /data.
- The TCP path sends measured 64 KiB chunks. The Telnet/Base64 fallback also
  reports bounded progress, and progress cannot move backwards if fallback is
  entered after a partial TCP attempt.
- The Home Assistant progress flow reserves 100% for a transfer that returned
  after the existing remote size and MD5 verification.
- The progress text includes the actual filename and "File 1 of 1".

Live hub transfer evidence:
- Target: Router 192.168.0.221.
- A generated 128044-byte WAV was uploaded through the exact v0.36.5 client.
- Elapsed time: 5.46 seconds.
- Hub MD5: e72118a91b6dce6f52161b4bff9f2352, identical to the source.
- Transfer callbacks reported 65536/128044, 128044/128044 and final success.
- The temporary /data/musics/music-ch/__codex_upload_probe.wav was removed and

Root cause fixed:
- The earlier upload stopped before data transfer in the multi-file capacity
  manifest command and timed out after 30 seconds. That is why no progress was
  visible and no WAV arrived.
- v0.36.5 removes that obsolete batch manifest from upload and uses the tested
  single-file capacity check. Multi-file deletion keeps its separate bounded
  manifest and is unchanged.

WAV catalog and deletion guarantees retained:
- /data/musics/music-us and all nested WAV files stay hidden and protected.
- Other confirmed WAV selections are deleted directly without backup archives.
- The complete deletion manifest is validated and the reported deleted count
